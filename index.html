Approach

Why an AI tutor, and how we get there

Unlimited patience

It explains the same step as many times as the student needs.

No judgment

A student can be wrong without feeling embarrassed in front of anyone.

Always available

After school, before a test, or at 9pm on a Sunday.

Face to face

Video and on-screen questions keep a student engaged. This is the hypothesis the PoC tests.

It can still get things wrong. In testing, Hana once praised a wrong answer. A prompt rule fixed it, and it is why the parent recap is a draft a person reviews before it is sent.

Customer journey

DRAFT Confirm the stages and criteria.
  1. Discovery

    Agree on the problem

    • Who buys: the school or the parent
    • What success looks like after 30 days
    • Stable student IDs so memory follows the student
    • Curriculum, and what parents need to see
    • Privacy: consent, data retention, EU students
  2. PoC You are here

    One grade, one course

    • Grade 5 fractions, 4 lessons
    • One tutor, Hana, with memory per student
    • Question cards and screen share
    • A parent recap after every session
  3. Pilot

    Real students, real numbers

    • A small group of Grade 5 students
    • Measure lessons finished and return visits
    • Parents rate the recaps
  4. Scale

    Every grade, every course

    • A tutor for each grade and course
    • Student accounts with stable IDs
    • Live session log and email delivery

Findings

Two things I would raise

Product feedback

Cards cannot show math fractions

A Grade 5 student needs to see fractions written the way their worksheet shows them. I tested three ways: slashes work, a Markdown rule draws a bar across the whole card, and code blocks are not supported. My ask to Tavus: math rendering on canvas cards. It would help every education customer.

Raise with the client

The end-of-call summary describes the student

After each call, Tavus writes a summary of what the camera saw. With emotion recognition set to limited, it still described my appearance, clothes, room and mood. In production the camera faces a child. The parent recap and memory stay about math only, but I would ask whether this summary can be turned off and whether zero data retention fits this client.
